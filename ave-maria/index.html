LATINE

Ave Maria, gratia plena, Dominus tecum, ave Maria.
Benedicta tu in mulieribus, et benedictus fructus ventris tui: Jesus.
Sancta Maria, ora pro nobis.

ENGLISH

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee, hail Mary.
Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb: Jesus.
Holy Mary, pray for us.

Arcadelt wrote the music as a three-voice French chanson, Nous voyons que les hommes; Dietsch fitted the Latin text and added the bass in the 1840s. Notes entered from the free edition by Casey Rule at NoteNova; dynamics as printed there.
