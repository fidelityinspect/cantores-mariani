Our mission

We exist to promote a culture of beauty and reverence in the Mass through the formation of vocalists and cantors who elevate sacred chant and polyphony to pride of place.

How to practice

  1. Pick your partYour notes turn blue, and the other voices play softly underneath.
  2. Learn a sectionChoose one phrase and it repeats until you have it.
  3. Tap any noteHear the pitch, and playback starts from there.
